About TruGrinn

Food should remain food.

Exceptional taste and ingredient integrity are equal priorities. Through thoughtful formulation, we strive to create products that people can genuinely enjoy with confidence.

Why COCOJAG?

A chocolate drink made the way food should be.

COCOJAG began with one simple question: "Would we happily serve this to our own children?" If the answer wasn't yes, it didn't belong. That simple principle guided every formulation decision.

We believe parents shouldn't have to choose between great taste and ingredients they can trust. COCOJAG is our attempt to prove that clean-label food can still be something children genuinely look forward to drinking.

What Makes COCOJAG Different

Every ingredient has a purpose.

Great products aren't created by adding more ingredients. They're created by choosing better ones. Every ingredient in COCOJAG has a reason to exist—whether it's improving taste, texture, nutrition or the overall drinking experience. Nothing is included simply because it makes manufacturing easier or cheaper.

Naturally Sweetened

Jaggery delivers the sweetness. Dates add depth. Together they create a fuller flavour without relying on refined white sugar.

Rich Chocolate Taste

Natural cocoa delivers the rich chocolate taste children love, balanced carefully for an enjoyable drink.

Clean Label

No artificial flavours, colours, additives or preservatives. Just ingredients chosen with intention.

Balanced for Everyday Enjoyment

Chocolate, sweetness and creaminess are carefully balanced to create a drink children genuinely enjoy while giving parents ingredients they can trust.
