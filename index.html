Why COCOJAG?

A chocolate drink made the way food should be.

COCOJAG began with one simple question: "Would we happily serve this to our own children?" If the answer wasn't yes, it didn't belong. That simple principle guided every formulation decision.

We believe parents shouldn't have to choose between great taste and ingredients they can trust. COCOJAG is our attempt to prove that clean-label food can still be something children genuinely look forward to drinking.

Our Story

How It All Began

Like many parents, we wanted to give our children foods they would genuinely enjoy while feeling confident about what went into them. When they asked for a chocolate drink, we began looking for one that brought together great taste and ingredients we felt good about serving.

We couldn't find quite what we were looking for, so we decided to create it ourselves. What started as a simple recipe in our own kitchen became a journey of continuous refinement.

Every ingredient was carefully considered. Every change had a purpose. The goal was never just to make another chocolate drink—it was to create one our own family would happily enjoy, day after day.

As friends and family began trying COCOJAG, they kept coming back for more. Their encouragement gave us the confidence to think beyond our own home.

That simple beginning became TruGrinn — because when true ingredients meet exceptional taste, they create genuine grins around the family table.
